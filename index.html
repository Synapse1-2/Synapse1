Synapse 12
Study Smarter. Go Further.
Version 1.0 · Class 9 to 12

Synapse 12 is one place for everything a school student needs: plan your day, focus without distractions, learn chapter by chapter, practise with quizzes and study together with friends in rooms.

Meet the maker
V
Vijay
17 · Class 12 student
Sikar, Rajasthan

I made Synapse 12 because I wanted one app that does everything I need for studying, and one that helps me stay away from distractions. Instead of jumping between five different apps, I wanted my plan, my timer, my syllabus, my practice and my study group in one calm place.

I built it step by step, one feature at a time, and I am still adding more. If you are a student like me, I hope it helps you too.

Your data

When you sign in with Google, your progress is saved to your account so it follows you to any phone. Guests keep everything on their own phone only.

Questions you ask Synapse AI are sent through a private server to an AI service to get an answer. Please do not type personal details. Each student gets a limited number of AI questions per day.

Room chats older than 30 days and chat files older than 7 days are removed from the server automatically. Files you open are saved on your own phone.

Make the most of Synapse 12

Start small. One 25 minute focus session today is better than a plan you never start.

Plan tomorrow tonight. Add 3 tasks before you sleep. Waking up with a clear list saves the first hour.

Learn from mistakes. After a quiz, read the explanations of the questions you got wrong. That is where the marks are.

Study with someone. Make a room with 2 or 3 friends, share notes and start a focus challenge. A little friendly competition helps.

Use AI as a tutor, not a shortcut. Try the question first, then ask Synapse AI to check your steps.

What is new in 1.0

Light and dark themes, study rooms with chat, whiteboard, notes and files, Synapse AI with photo doubts, progress sync across phones, and a new logo.
